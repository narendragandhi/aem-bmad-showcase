# Business Requirements

This document outlines the high-level business and functional requirements for the AEM as a Cloud Service project.

## Business Goals

- Launch a new marketing website to showcase our products.
- Improve content authoring efficiency and governance.
- Provide a responsive and accessible user experience across all devices.
- Integrate with our existing CRM and analytics platforms.

## Functional Requirements

- **Content Authoring**:
    - A library of reusable components (e.g., hero, carousel, text with image).
    - A flexible template system for creating new pages.
    - An approval workflow for publishing content.
- **Digital Asset Management**:
    - Centralized repository for all marketing assets.
    - AI-powered smart tagging for images.
    - Asset metadata management.
- **Personalization**:
    - Ability to personalize content based on user location.
    - A/B testing capabilities for key landing pages.
- **SEO**:
    - Customizable page titles, meta descriptions, and canonical URLs.
    - Automatic generation of sitemaps.
